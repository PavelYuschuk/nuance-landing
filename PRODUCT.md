# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vanilla HTML5 / CSS3 / JavaScript (Vite) + SmoothScroll.js

## Users

Tactical gaming community, competitive gamers, esports enthusiasts, and visitors evaluating or joining the NUANCE tactical squad.

## Product Purpose

A high-end, immersive web landing page for **NUANCE** — a tactical quick-reaction squad. It communicates squad identity, operating philosophy, historical genesis, player roster, and interactive mechanics.

## Positioning

"Surgical precision over mass numbers. Asymmetric strategy, adaptability, and rapid reaction where mass community inertia stalls."

## Operating Context

Desktop and mobile browsers with smooth scroll integration, high visual clarity, dynamic HUD micro-interactions, dark ambient aesthetic, and zero intrusive UI clutter.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Section 3.1 Header**: Floating capsule glassmorphism header with active section underline glow and language switcher.
- **Section 3.2 Hero**: High-impact central statement with parallax scroll fade.
- **Section 3.3 About (Genesis)**: 3-layer sticky scroll architecture (`3.3.0 Кто мы`, `3.3.1 История`, `3.3.2 Механизм`) with 3-segment progress tracker and white outline card frame.
- **Section 3.4 Team**: Full-viewport snap scroll screens for players (`Presa4ek`, `Art1zi`, `Matvi4`) with 9:16 aspect ratio placeholders, asymmetric layout, glowing player titles, and fixed Global HUD ring indicator (Scroll Spy).
- **Git Policy**: Strict manual commit/push rule. Automated `git commit` or `git push` is forbidden.

## Brand Commitments

- **Base Colors**: `#0F0F0F` (Dark Base), `#141C29` (Surface), `#27354D` (Border), `#FFFFFF` (Primary Text), `#888888` (Secondary Text).
- **Player Accents**: Presa4ek (`#C1FF00`), Art1zi (`#00E5FF`), Matvi4 (`#FFC107`).
- **Typography**: `Rajdhani` (Headings H1-H3), `JetBrains Mono` (Body/Nav), `Sofia Sans Semi Condensed` (UI/Tags).

## Evidence on Hand

- Production codebase in `index.html`, `src/styles/`, `src/scripts/`.
- PRD v.1.1 design specifications.
- Assets in `public/assets/images/`.

## Product Principles

1. **Precision & Speed**: Frictionless interaction with intuitive information hierarchy.
2. **Visual Excellence**: Premium dark aesthetic with curated color accents and typography details.
3. **Responsive Fluidity**: Seamless behavior across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices without content shifting.
